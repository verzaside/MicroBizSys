# Guia Rápido → BackOffice OS Funnel

## Canonical role of the guides

Niche-specific **Guias Rápidos** are the education/acquisition layer for the BackOffice OS portfolio.

Each guide is a standalone educational publication that may be sold without the system. It must deliver value to readers who never buy the OS. Its commercial role is both independent literature and a natural funnel to the system; it is not the system's user manual.

They are not a separate unrelated business and should not become a competing spreadsheet/template product line.

Canonical funnel:

```text
Guia Rápido
    ↓
BackOffice OS
```

Do not add an intermediate “Kit” product by default.

## Guide purpose

Each guide should:
- be genuinely useful standalone;
- teach the operating method and concepts;
- help the reader understand what to track and why;
- use conceptual examples;
- naturally surface the pain of managing multiple customers, jobs, deliverables, costs, rights, payments, stock, or margins.

The guide should be “OS-aware” without becoming a disguised sales brochure.

## Boundary

The guide teaches the operating method, concepts and judgment independently of software.

The OS helps apply that method through guided workflows. Separate product help explains screens, buttons, validation behavior and exact backup/restore steps.

Keep product invitations brief and clearly separated from instruction. Removing every product mention must leave a useful, coherent guide. Do not withhold essential educational content to force an OS purchase.

Guides should avoid distributing fully functional calculators, management templates, or operational workbooks that materially substitute for the paid OS.

## Development alignment

When a guide chapter describes a real workflow, that workflow should inform:
- domain model;
- UI flow;
- validation rules;
- terminology;
- onboarding.

Write core guide sections alongside each workflow slice. Deliver the relevant explanation, worked example and system scenario together so the product owner can read the guide while validating the system. Resolve discrepancies in terminology, calculations and behavior in both artifacts.

Do not wait for a complete publication before implementing, and do not defer all core guide writing until the product is finished. Publication design and final editing can follow workflow validation. Track guide review separately from system tests; draft text must not imply unimplemented functionality exists.

For unfamiliar niches, verify industry-specific factual claims using appropriate primary sources. Owner review checks understanding and usability; it does not replace domain evidence. See the [guide workflow and coverage map](../guides/README.md).
