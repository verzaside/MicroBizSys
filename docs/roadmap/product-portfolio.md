# Product Portfolio Roadmap

## Purpose

Keep the full mapped niche list alive while building the first product. This is the product opportunity and launch-candidate roadmap; [Development Plan](development-plan.md) describes the technical sequence.

UGC is the approved first build. Personalizados and Maquete complete the initial reference wave; Food is a high-interest follow-on. Subsequent launch order and dates are not committed. Validate each product before expanding implementation scope.

## Mapped niches

## Maintaining the roadmap

Review after the first product's validation and when new customer evidence arrives. Preserve candidates even when deferred; record changes of status and the reason instead of silently removing them.

Before promoting a candidate into an implementation/release plan, record:
- the target customer, main pain, and first useful end-to-end workflow;
- reusable modules and the smallest niche-specific additions;
- whether mobile is essential at launch, the required actions, and how they will be validated;
- evidence from prospective customers or beta use;
- expected support burden and fit with one-time-purchase economics;
- the next validation step and the reason for its priority.

Do not invent launch dates, market scores, or validated demand. Newly recovered niche ideas from earlier research should be added with their source and validation status.
