# ADR-003 — One-Time Purchase as Core Commercial Model

**Status:** Accepted

## Decision

Core products are sold as one-time purchases: one and done, with no subscriptions or planned paid upgrades.

## Rationale

Target customers often dislike recurring SaaS fees and are underserved between spreadsheets and full ERP/SaaS.

## Consequences

- support minutes per sale must remain low;
- no core dependency on vendor-hosted infrastructure;
- versioning and bug-fix delivery must not introduce a paid-upgrade model;
- support duration and release cadence remain unspecified;
- product quality, onboarding, and diagnostics are essential.
